# WFS PROJECT v2.4 — local spatial asset license inventory

Eight GLB meshes were **authored procedurally for WFS PROJECT** using `tools/build_original_assets.py`, which is included for reproducibility. No third-party or downloaded meshes, textures, logos or animation packs were incorporated. This project's authored meshes may be used, modified and redistributed under **CC0 1.0 Universal**, to the extent copyright applies. Generator uses `trimesh` (MIT) and `numpy` (BSD-3-Clause) as build-time development dependencies; those libraries are not bundled inside the GLBs or used at runtime.

All GLBs are glTF 2.0 indexed meshes with local vertex colors and `pbrMetallicRoughness` factors. The dependency-free renderer evaluates a single-light Cook-Torrance-style specular approximation and ambient fill; it does **not** include realistic reflection probes, texture maps, skeletal skinning, post-processing, shadow mapping or photorealistic illumination. Character movement is procedural; **it is not a rigged skeletal animation**. Original local assets have no external network dependencies. The CPU renderer uses a simpler shading approximation and is not visually equivalent to WebGL2.

Asset rendering, licenses and source generation must be revalidated when third-party replacement meshes are introduced.
